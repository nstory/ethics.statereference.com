What is a conflict of interest disclosure?

The Massachusetts Conflict of Interest Law (G.L. c. 268A) requires that public employees file certain disclosures when a conflict of interest could be present. Examples include: travel being reimbursed by a foreign entity, having a financial interest related to your position, or otherwise having the appearance of a conflict of interest.

The conflict of interest law is administered and enforced by the State Ethics Commission. The commission is the custodian for disclosures directly filed with the commission, however, the vast majority of state employees file their disclosures with their appointing authorities.

What is this database?

StateReference Ethics is a compilation of these disclosures made into a searchable database.

We submit public records requests to agencies for the disclosures filed with them. We then OCR the documents and upload them here.
