What is this?

The Massachusetts Conflict of Interest Law (G.L. c. 268A) requires that public employees file certain disclosures when a conflict of interest could be present. Examples include: being reimbursed for travel expenses, having a financial interest related to your position, or otherwise having the appearance of a conflict of interest.

The conflict of interest law is administered by the State Ethics Commission. The commission is the custodian for disclosures directly filed with the commission, however, the vast majority of state employees file their disclosures with their appointing authorities.

The goal of this database is to collect these disclosures in a single place and make them searchable.
